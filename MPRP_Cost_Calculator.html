Important notes:
  • All fees are indicative and subject to change by the government of Malta. This calculator should be read alongside the full proposal document.
  • GCS professional fee starts at €25,000 for a main applicant and increases by €500 per additional dependant beyond a spouse/partner and two children under 18.
  • † Government contribution of €7,500 applies per adult dependant (aged 18+) other than a spouse or partner; this is not applicable to minor dependants.
  • Admin fee totals €60,000, paid in two instalments: €15,000 at the start of the application (Step 02) and €45,000 post-approval (Step 03). Admin fee, real estate, government contribution and philanthropic donation are all required for eligibility — real estate options are purchase (min. €375,000) or rental (min. €14,000/yr).
  • Asset requirement: minimum €500,000 in assets, of which €150,000 must be financial assets, or minimum €650,000 in assets, of which €75,000 must be financial assets.
  • Temporary residence cards in Step 02 are optional; residence permit renewal every 5 years carries a government cost of €500.
  • The 12-month timeline is a guideline only and is at the discretion of the relevant authorities.
